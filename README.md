# 👋 About Me

I’m **Ines Amirat**, a Software Engineer and AI/ML/NLP enthusiast currently completing a  
**Master’s degree in Software Engineering at the University of Montpellier (France).**

I specialize in:
- **Backend engineering** (Java, Spring Boot, REST architectures)  
- **Frontend development** (Angular, TypeScript, Tailwind)  
- **Applied AI & NLP** (text processing, ML pipelines, classification, medical data)  

I enjoy designing systems that are:
✔ Clean  
✔ Modular  
✔ Scalable  
✔ Scientifically rigorous  

Currently, I am deepening my expertise in **Machine Learning, NLP, and medical data processing** while developing strong full-stack engineering foundations.
